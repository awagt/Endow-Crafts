The creative studio

Creativity Behind Every Detail.

Design, Cricut cutting, engraving and hand-finishing all happen in the studio, so every detail is checked before it reaches you.

  1. 01

    Designed & cut with Cricut

    Names, dates, logos and Arabic or English lettering are laid out, then cut with precision in vinyl, heat-transfer and paper.

  2. 02

    Engraved to last

    Wood, acrylic and leather are engraved with fine detail, from a single name to a full logo run.

  3. 03

    Finished by hand

    Every piece is checked, assembled and packed in the studio, with named tags and message cards.

  4. 04

    Ready to gift

    Wrapped, tied and ready for collection or delivery across Qatar.

Meet the founder

Fathima Safra

Founder, Endow Crafts · Cricut Brand Ambassador — Middle East

Endow Crafts was founded by Fathima Safra, who brings a maker's love of crafting to every personalised order the studio takes on, from a single name mug to a full corporate run.

As a Cricut Brand Ambassador in the Middle East, Safra also runs the studio's Cricut workshops, teaching beginners, families and teams to design and make pieces of their own.
